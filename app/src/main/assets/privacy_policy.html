Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

FlySafe Weather collects the following information to provide accurate weather and flight safety data:

  • Location data (GPS coordinates)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • GNSS/GPS satellite data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide accurate local weather information
  • Display relevant TFRs (Temporary Flight Restrictions)
  • Calculate flight risk assessments
  • Improve app functionality and user experience

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not store or transmit your location data to any servers except when necessary to fetch weather information from official weather services.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Aviation Weather Center (for METAR data)
  • FAA Services (for TFR information)
  • Google Maps (for location visualization)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data
  • Opt out of location tracking
  • Disable specific features

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@flysafeweather.app

8. Disclaimer

FlySafe Weather is not an official aviation tool. The weather information and flight safety assessments provided are for informational purposes only and should not be used as the sole source for flight planning or decision-making.
